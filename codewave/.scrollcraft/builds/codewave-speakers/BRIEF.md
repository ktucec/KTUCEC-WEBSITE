# BRIEF: CODEWAVE 2026, speakers section

Self-authored under explicit creative delegation ("Tasarımın nasıl olması gerektiğini belirlemek senin
sorumluluğun"; "Konuşmacıların yer aldığı section'ı ... tamamen yeniden tasarla"). Scope is ONE section of an
existing Next.js page (dark gala direction pinned by the user), not a whole site.

## 1. Vibe
Authored: a stage at doors-open. Dark hall, one cold light, names arriving. References: a theatre roll call
before curtain, festival line-up posters, a film's opening credits over black.

## 2. Journey (user's words: "ilk ekrandan son konuşmacıya kadar tutarlı")
Hall is dark, the question is asked → each speaker steps into the light, one at a time → each one takes a seat
in the line-up → the line-up completes itself, one seat still empty → release into the rest of the page.

## 3. Energy curve
Calm open, rising rhythm through five arrivals, one held high point at the assembled line-up, calm release.

## 4. Feeling curve
1  Anticipation  dark stage, a single beam, the question "Sahnede kimler var" assembles
2  Recognition   speaker one steps through a slit of light, the name huge behind them
3  Momentum      speakers two to five arrive from alternating sides, each docks into the line-up
4  Completion    the five docked portraits rise and spread into the full line-up, the sixth seat glows empty (PEAK)
5  Release       the stage lets go, the line-up stays as the last image

Peak (what a visitor tells a friend): "every speaker walked into the light and took their seat, and at the end
the whole line-up was sitting there with one empty chair left."
Act: 4. It gets the most scroll room and the largest visual change; act 3 before it is a steady rhythm.

It's the site where the line-up assembles itself while you scroll.

## 5. Signature move
The DOCK: after each spotlight, the portrait itself flies from centre stage into its seat in the line-up rail
(measured positions, not a crossfade). Five flights build the roster that becomes the peak.

## 6. Range
Within the page's pinned dark-gala direction: restrained, cinematic, no new palette.

## 7. Structure
Distinct scenes inside one held stage (pinned) on desktop. Phone: no pin, a flow of full-height arrivals with a
sticky seat bar filling in (same story, own composition).

## 8. Assets
None real yet. Speaker photos arrive later from the client. Test portraits are synthetic, visibly marked
"ÖRNEK", never photoreal faces (real people's names must not be attached to invented faces).

## Authored silence
Act 1 holds ~0.35 of a viewport with only the beam and the question: intentional quiet before the first arrival.

## Score (devices per act)
| Act | Device | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Anticipation | kinetic (word mask rise) + pin ground | the question assembles while nothing else moves |
| 2-3 Arrivals | reveal (slit aperture) + parallax planes (name behind, portrait mid, plate front) | stepping into light is a change of state |
| 3 Dock | bespoke DOCK flight (signature) | the line-up is built by the reader's hand |
| 4 Peak | pan-style spread of the rail into the full line-up | lateral spread reads as "the whole cast" |
| 5 Release | flow (un-pin, line-up remains) | the last image holds |
| Phone | flow + scrubbed reveal per arrival + sticky seat bar | no pin on touch; the bar is the dock |
Pointer: subtle tilt on the active portrait (fine pointer only).
Reduced motion: no pin, no flights; a static line-up grid with every name, title and company.
